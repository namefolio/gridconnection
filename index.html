01 · The opportunity

Why this name, and why now

Britain's electricity grid is the bottleneck of the energy transition. Solar farms, battery sites, data centres, housing estates and EV hubs all need a connection before they can do anything, and in many regions the wait has stretched into the 2030s.

  • The queue has been reset. In December 2025 NESO cut a 722 GW connection queue down to 283 GW of prioritised offers. Developers are now replanning, re-applying and looking for faster routes.
  • Record network investment. Ofgem has approved £24bn as the first step of an estimated £80bn programme to expand the grid between 2026 and 2031.
  • Demand is surging. Contracted demand connections tripled from 41 GW to 125 GW in seven months, led by data centres.

Every one of those changes sends developers, landowners and businesses searching for clear, trustworthy help with grid connections. This domain is that phrase, on the UK's own extension, with nothing added.

Why it works

  • It is the term of art. Ofgem, NESO, the network operators and the trade press all call it a grid connection. Your visitors already use those exact words.

  • It covers the whole category. Applications, offers, upgrades, costs and timelines all fit under one name.
